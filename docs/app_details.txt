# Smart Dukan (Smart Store) - Project Details

## 1. Project Overview
Smart Dukan is a professional-grade, cloud-native Point of Sale (POS) and Inventory Management system built with Flutter and Supabase. It is designed to modernize shop operations by providing a robust, multi-branch capable platform for sales, stock tracking, and supplier management.

## 2. Primary Uses
- **Retail Operations**: Streamlining daily sales and invoice generation.
- **Inventory Control**: Real-time tracking of stock levels across multiple branches.
- **Supplier Management**: Managing vendor relationships and procurement.
- **Business Intelligence**: Analyzing sales performance and stock health through a centralized dashboard.

## 3. Technology Stack
- **Frontend**: Flutter (3.x)
- **State Management**: flutter_bloc (9.x) with Equatable
- **Navigation**: GoRouter (17.x)
- **Backend**: Supabase (PostgreSQL, Storage, Auth)
- **Reporting**: pdf & printing packages for invoice generation
- **Data Handling**: excel & csv packages for import/export
- **UI/UX**: Google Fonts (Inter, Manrope), Skeletonizer for loading states

## 4. Core Features & Functions

### 🏢 Multi-Branch Architecture
- **Branch Switching**: Ability to manage multiple physical or logical store locations from a single application.
- **Service Layer**: Managed via `BranchService` with local persistence using SharedPreferences.

### 🛒 Sales & POS Terminal
- **Multi-item Invoicing**: Support for adding multiple items to a single transaction with real-time tax and total calculations.
- **PDF Generation**: Automated creation of professional invoices for printing or digital sharing.
- **Manual Sales**: Interface for recording custom or quick transactions that bypass the standard inventory catalog.

### 📦 Intelligent Inventory
- **Full CRUD**: Comprehensive management of products, including categories and pricing.
- **Cloud-Native Media**: Product images are stored in Supabase Storage with built-in cropping and optimization.
- **Low Stock Alerts**: Visual indicators and notifications for items below safety thresholds.
- **Category Management**: Hierarchical organization of inventory items.

### 🤝 Supplier & Purchase Module
- **Supplier Tracking**: Management of vendor contact details and performance history.
- **Stock Upload Sheets**: Dedicated interface for recording incoming stock and updating inventory levels.
- **Purchase Auditing**: Historical log of all procurement transactions.

## 5. Built to Date (Implemented Modules)

### Dashboard Feature
- `DashboardScreen`: Displays key performance indicators, sales charts, and operational summaries.

### Inventory Feature
- `InventoryScreen`: The main catalog view with search and filter capabilities.
- `CategoryManagementScreen`: Interface for managing product groupings.
- `ItemPriceScreen`: Global pricing and markup management tool.

### Sales Feature
- `SalesScreen`: The primary POS interface used by staff for checkout.
- `ManualSaleScreen`: Versatile entry form for quick or non-standard sales.

### Purchase Feature
- `PurchaseScreen`: Audit log of all inventory acquisitions.
- `AddPurchaseScreen`: Flow for recording new stock arrivals from suppliers.
- `ManualStockUploadSheet`: Utility for rapid manual stock updates.

### Supplier Feature
- `SupplierScreen`: Centralized management of the supplier database.

### Auth & Security
- `LoginScreen` & `SignupScreen`: Secure authentication flows with Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) integration.

## 6. Architecture & Patterns
- **Layered Design**: Follows a feature-based structure with clear separation of `view`, `bloc`, `widgets`, and `models`.
- **Dependency Injection**: Services (Auth, Branch, API) are initialized globally for consistent access.
- **Reactive UI**: State-driven interface ensuring immediate feedback to user actions.
